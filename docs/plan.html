The rules this page follows
  1. Sequence, don't sacrifice. Everything in the bucket gets a season. Only the order is a decision.
  2. Few things at once. One main milestone holds the deep blocks. A second is fine only in a small side slot, and best from a different area of life.
  3. Short, protected depth. One to two focused hours before the day's obligations beat scattered evenings.
  4. A hard stop. After shutdown the day is over, and between seasons you take a week off.

This page edits seasons.yaml. Progress (logged minutes, finished milestones, rest weeks) is written by the engine and lw season, and shown here read-only.
